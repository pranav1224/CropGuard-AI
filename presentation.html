Frontend Design Philosophy: Aesthetic, Functional & Farmer-Centric

Lush Agri Palette

Emerald (#059669), Forest Green, and Harvest Gold palette designed for psychological resonance in agricultural contexts.

Glassmorphic Cards

Semi-translucent white cards (rgba(255,255,255,0.92)) with backdrop-filter blur and soft emerald ambient shadows to eliminate visual clutter.

Replaces dense scientific spreadsheets with reactive visual sliders, color-coded severity meters, and 1-click inspection chamber presets.

Responsive Layout

Tailwind CSS flex and grid systems ensuring seamless usability across low-cost rural smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations.

Future Development Roadmap: Expanding CropGuard Capabilities

P1

Drone & Satellite NDVI

Ingestion of multispectral drone footage to compute Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) for whole-field aerial stress detection before ground symptoms emerge.

P2

Offline PWA & ONNX

Zero-bandwidth rural operation using Progressive Web App service workers and client-side INT8 quantized MobileNetV3 running on ONNX WebAssembly.

P3

Multilingual Voice AI

Voice-in, voice-out advisory across Hindi, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, and Bengali to empower low-literacy farmers with spoken diagnosis and treatment readouts.

P4

IoT Smart Fertigation

Hardware integration with in-situ soil probes streaming telemetry via MQTT, automatically opening solenoid drip irrigation and liquid fertilizer valves.
